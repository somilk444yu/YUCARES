How is YU different from ordinary air?

Ordinary atmospheric air is only 21% oxygen and 78% nitrogen. YU provides 99% pure medical-grade oxygen. Each breath carries nearly five times the oxygen concentration of ambient air, giving your body immediate revitalization during elevation or exertion.

Each canister provides roughly 150 to 200 short breaths, which equals 15 to 20 sessions of 5 to 10 breaths each. Use it whenever air feels thin, during workouts, or after commuting through heavy traffic.
