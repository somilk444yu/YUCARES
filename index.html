How many breaths in a can?

Each canister provides roughly 150 to 200 short inhalations, equivalent to around 15 to 20 personal recovery sessions.

Is it safe for older parents on yatra?

Yes. YU was designed to be lightweight, simple, and operable with one hand. Ideal for parents visiting high-altitude pilgrimage destinations like Kedarnath or Badrinath. Consult a doctor beforehand if they have pre-existing cardiovascular or respiratory conditions.

Can I carry it on a flight?

Commercial airlines restrict pressurised canisters in passenger luggage. For Leh, Ladakh, and similar trips, arrange delivery directly to your destination hotel.
