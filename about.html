Our Mission: Cleaner-Feeling Breathing for Urban India

YU was created for people who spend their days in traffic, offices, gyms, co-working spaces, and high-rise apartments. We believe that how you breathe affects how you feel—not just during workouts, but throughout your everyday life.

Why YU Exists

In India’s biggest cities, air that looks “fine” can still feel heavy, dusty, and tiring. We saw friends and colleagues reach for coffee, energy drinks, and air purifiers—but not much that helped their breathing feel lighter in the moment.

YU is our response: a portable wellness tool that gives people a simple way to support more comfortable breathing in the middle of real life—without drama, hype, or medical claims.

What we care about

  • Honest, clear communication without fear-based messaging.
  • Products that feel premium, minimal, and calming to use.
  • Responsible positioning that respects medical boundaries.

The Simple Idea Behind YU Oxygen Can

Our air is a mix of oxygen, nitrogen, and other gases—plus particles from pollution, dust, and smoke. In crowded cities and closed rooms, the air can feel heavy and stale.

YU Oxygen Can offers high-purity oxygen in a controlled format, giving you moments of cleaner-feeling breathing when your surroundings feel less than ideal.

Science, without the jargon

We keep our science simple: no exaggerated promises, no complex technical language—just straightforward oxygen support designed for comfort.

We work with qualified partners on filling, safety, and quality checks, and we communicate in plain language so you always know what you’re using and why.

Wellness, Not Medicine

We take our responsibility seriously. YU does not promote YU Oxygen Can as a cure, treatment, or medical solution. We design our products for people who are already mindful of their wellness and want an additional breathing comfort option in their day.

We encourage all customers to listen to their bodies, follow safety guidance, and always seek professional medical advice when needed.

YU Oxygen Can is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
