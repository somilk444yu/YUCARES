What’s Inside and Why It Matters

YU Oxygen Can is filled with high-purity oxygen in a precisely sealed 12L can. It’s designed for short, intentional breathing sessions whenever your surroundings feel heavy, stale, or tiring— without wires, batteries, or complex devices.

How to Use YU Oxygen Can

Use YU when you want your breathing to feel fresher, lighter, or more spacious—during mid-day slumps, after a workout, or post-commute.

Hold the can upright, bring the mouthpiece close to your mouth, press the lever gently, and breathe normally for a short period. Use it in well-ventilated areas and avoid pressing the lever continuously for long stretches.

Quick usage highlights

  • Hold upright and keep away from face when not in use.
  • Place mouthpiece near your mouth, not inside it.
  • Press lever gently—short bursts are enough.
  • Breathe normally; avoid holding your breath.

Safety, Storage & Everyday Care

Is YU a medical device?

No. YU Oxygen Can is a wellness and comfort product. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

How should I store the can?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and open flames. Do not puncture or burn the can, even when empty.

Can I use it in a closed room?

Use in a well-ventilated space. Avoid using near open flames, smoking areas, or high heat.

Is it safe to travel with?

Check with your airline or transport provider before carrying pressurised cans. Follow all local regulations when travelling.

How often can I use it?

Use in moderation as part of your wellness routine. If you have any medical conditions, consult a healthcare professional before use.

Who Should Not Use YU

If you have chronic respiratory conditions, heart disease, or any serious medical diagnosis, or if you are pregnant, nursing, or under 18 years of age, consult a qualified healthcare professional before using YU Oxygen Can.

Do not use YU as a replacement for prescribed treatment, medication, or medical oxygen. If you experience discomfort, dizziness, chest pain, or breathing difficulty, stop using the product and seek medical help immediately.
