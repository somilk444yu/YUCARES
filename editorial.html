FAQ

Straight answers.

Is YU a medical device?

No. YU is a wellness product. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it does not replace acclimatisation, rest or descent.

How many breaths in a can?

Roughly 150 to 200 short breaths, around 15 to 20 sessions of 5 to 10 breaths.

Is it okay for older parents?

It is light and works with one hand. Anyone with heart, lung or blood-pressure conditions should speak to a doctor before going to altitude.

Can I fly with it?

Airlines restrict pressurised canisters in passenger luggage. For Leh and similar trips, have it delivered to your hotel.
